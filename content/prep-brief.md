## A. Working MTP draft options (2–3) + litmus tests

> MTP = Massive Transformative Purpose. Prefer **protocol** over poster (ExO / Founder OS lineage): statement + what you refuse + who it’s not for.  
> Personal MTP ≠ AFS firm MTP stub (AFS: buses/trust/speed under Ramzi judgment). Keep firm MTP and personal MTP distinct; use RE/AFS as **labs**, not the whole purpose.

### Option 1 — Opportunity routing (primary working draft)

**Statement:** *Rewire how opportunity finds the right humans — so talent, capital, and customers compound into abundance instead of sitting trapped in networks and inboxes.*

| Litmus | Pass if… | Fail if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Endorsement** | You’d green-light a day’s work that increases *flow of qualified opportunity* to people you care about (buyers, sellers, partners) | Day’s work is clever tooling with no opportunity moved |
| **Refusal** | You refuse: another internal enablement seat, pure feature factory, “AI for AI’s sake,” roles that wall you from customers | You take the seat because it’s prestigious / stable / AI-branded |
| **Identity** | Stay because judgment on *who gets what shot* is the work; leave if you’re reduced to ticket-closer or silent IC | Can’t say who this is *not* for |

**Disqualifiers (Identity):** PE ops theater; “AI team of 3 with no customer”; roles that punish relationship-first operators.

### Option 2 — Relationship infrastructure for abundance

**Statement:** *Build the relationship layer that turns weak ties into compounding opportunity — memory, trust, and timing as infrastructure for abundance.*

| Litmus | Pass if… | Fail if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Endorsement** | Work that makes follow-through + intro quality measurably better (memryOS-class, Cloze-class, Ninja cadence) | CRM busywork with no deal/relationship outcome |
| **Refusal** | Refuse: surveillance-y growth hacks, spray outreach, tools that replace judgment instead of amplifying it | “More sequences” without trust/timing |
| **Identity** | You’re the person who *connects and remembers*; not the person who only ships pipes | Pure eng identity required to belong |

**Bridge to memryOS:** Treat memryOS as one *instance* of this MTP, not the MTP itself. `[WORKING]`

### Option 3 — Dual-domain operator → abundance OS `[SPECULATIVE` framing]
