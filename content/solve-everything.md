## 2. Core Thesis (accurate to source)

### Central question
ASI/superintelligence is no longer “if” but **who aims it, and at what**. Route large-scale intelligence at **Positive-Sum Moonshots** with rigorous, adversarially tested targets → (1) entire domains solved in bulk; (2) problems become **compute-bound**.

### Three foundational claims (Chapter 2)

1. **Cognition is a Commodity**  
   Input-based / hours-worked economy dies. Model quality past human limits + unit cost of cognition → electricity floor + friction of integration → 0. Intelligence becomes a utility you “pour” onto problems. New bottleneck = **routing** (where to aim). Leaders track **RoCS (Return on Cognitive Spend)** — value per token/compute dollar — not hours.

2. **Targeting Systems Industrialize Progress**  
   Industries need precise “this number = success” (not artisan taste). Targets + escrowed prizes route capital/R&D. Examples: Time-to-Drug-Approval; Learning Gain per Hour (LG/H). Policymakers should stand up **Targeting Authorities**, define metric, escrow prize, get out of the “how.”

3. **The “Shaped-Charge” Model**  
   Treat ASI like explosive energy that must be **focused** through Moonshots (positive-sum, auditable, composable). Safety improves when every step is measured against guardrails. Investors: don’t buy commoditizing models; buy **Primitives** (targeting systems, audit trails, payment rails).

### What “solved” means
- **Theoretical:** bottleneck shifts from human genius → available compute.  
- **Operational:** tasks beat human experts reliably, with transparent failure modes.  
Examples: Solved Math (proof in hours); Solved Physics (gap → theory → experiment design); Solved Biology (sequence → binder → cure recipe in 24h).

### Industrial Intelligence Stack (Chapter 3) — 9 layers
A domain cannot be industrialized until these are solid:

1. **Purpose and Payoff** — quantifiable goal (not vague mission)  
2. **Task Taxonomy** — map of tiny measurable actions  
3. **Observability** — sensors, logs, data streams  
4. **Targeting System (Harness)** — hard tests that try to break models  
5. **Model Layer** — the AI agent(s)  
6. **Actuation** — robots, APIs, contracts that affect the world  
7. **Verification and Red Teaming** — continuous independent attack  
8. **Governance and Incentives** — pay-for-performance, not hours  
9. **Distribution and Maintenance** — utility-grade scale/reliability  

(Glossary also lists these nine.) Diamandis Metatrends sometimes compresses to 7; **essay/glossary = 9**.

### Maturation Curve L0–L5 (six stages)

| Level | Name | Essence |
|-------|------|---------|
| **L0** | Ill-Posed / “The Muddle” | Contested objectives, messy data, anecdote/politics; AI can’t aim |
| **L1** | Measurable | Scoreboards exist; humans graded; AI as referee |
| **L2** | Repeatable | SOPs/checklists; AI assists (templates) |
| **L3** | Automated | Checklists → code; AI does ~80% volume; humans handle exceptions |
| **L4** | Industrialized | Buy **verified outcomes**; unit economics beat humans; humans audit/architect |
| **L5** | Commoditized / “Solved” | Compute-bound; compete on price; boring as tap water (e.g. gene sequencing) |

### Rails vs The Muddle
- **The Muddle:** bureaucracy, input-based pricing, scarcity-minded institutions; thrives on friction; can capture ASI for ads/grant theater/red tape.  
- **The Rails:** targeting platforms, audit harnesses, data trusts, action networks, compute escrow, outcome procurement — infrastructure that industrializes discovery.  
Race: build rails fast enough vs Muddle throttles ASI.

### 18-month window (“Regulatory Foundry Window” / Foundry Window)
~**18 months** while standards, data rights, and supply chains are still “molten.” Decisions lock path dependencies for decades (QWERTY analogy). Pressures: Lock-In of first credible targeting systems; supply-chain “seats” (energy, water, packaging); synthetic-data economics; cultural first impressions of AI.

### Bright / Muddled / Dark paths (Chapter 4)

1. **Bright Path (Abundance Machine):** Stack built; Moonshots proliferate; outcome-based procurement; by 2035 entire fields largely “solved.”  
2. **Muddle Path (Stagnation):** Fragmented standards; SI used for ads/spam/better grant proposals; margin expansion without public good.  
3. **Dark Path (The Freeze):** Safety incident → panic/policy freeze/capital flight; physical constraints bite; shadow AI markets; Moonshots unaddressed.

### Abundance Flywheel (Chapter 6)
Commitment (escrowed compute) → Focus (clear target) → Domain Collapse → Surplus → Reinvestment (next harder target), with programmatic safety downshifts.

### Solution Wavefront (Chapter 5) — sequence matters
- **Phase 1 (2026–2027):** Pure information — Math, CS/code, much of Physics  
- **Phase 2 (2028–2031):** Physical world — Chemistry/Materials, then Biology (Virtual Cell as trigger)  
- **Phase 3 (2032–2035):** Planetary systems — Energy, infrastructure, off-world  

### Prologue vignettes (texture, not prediction claims)
- **2026 Lock-In:** RoCS > EBITDA; Spec-to-Artifact; Replication Packs; Targeting Authorities; LG/H schools  
- **2030 Liquefaction:** Action Networks; Virtual Cell; Organ Abundance; fusion pilots; BCIs; Exolinguistics; Explorer of Purpose

## 3. The 15 Moonshots (Chapter 7 — official list)

*From solveeverything.org only. Metatrends paraphrase differs; do not substitute.*

Each is a “mega-XPRIZE” / shaped charge meant to force domain industrialization via **spillover** (solving the hardest problem builds tools for the rest of the field). Moonshot criteria: **Positive-Sum, Auditable, Composable**.

1. **Organ Abundance** — Industrialize regenerative medicine to manufacture human organs on demand.  
2. **Double Human Healthspan** — Industrialize longevity therapeutics toward Longevity Escape Velocity; goal ≥ doubling healthy lifespan.  
3. **End Hunger with Synthetic Food Systems** — Industrialize agriculture to decouple food production from land and weather.  
4. **AI-Empowered Education for All** — Industrialize and personalize pedagogy; democratize world-class teaching.  
5. **High-Bandwidth Brain-Computer Interfaces** — High-fidelity bidirectional cortex↔digital link; merge thought with machine speed.  
6. **Demonstrate Human Mind Uploading** — Substrate independence: digitize connectome, run on silicon.  
7. **Interspecies Communication & Uplift** — Decode “statistical grammar” of non-human intelligence; two-way communication / cognitive bridging.  
8. **Understanding Human Consciousness** — Move consciousness from philosophy to falsifiable mechanistic science.  
9. **Disaster Prediction & Avoidance** — Planetary Situational Awareness; predict and neutralize catastrophes before they hit.  
10. **Steward & Upgrade Earth’s Ecosystems** — Industrialize planetary ecology; reverse biosphere degradation; manage carbon; pathway toward substrate independence for sentient life.  
11. **Clean-Energy Abundance** — Infinite carbon-free baseload via terrestrial fusion physics + industrialized space solar capture.  
12. **Functional High-Logical-Qubit Quantum Computers** — Stabilize quantum info against noise; enable Quantum-Native Intelligence.  
13. **True Nanotechnology** — Atomically Precise Manufacturing (APM): programmable molecular machines with atomic precision.  
14. **Permanent Human Expansion into Earth–Moon–Mars–Asteroid ecosystem** — Industrialize the solar system; self-sustaining multi-planetary civilization.  
15. **Solve Fundamental Physics** — Grand Unified Theory; unify GR and QM.

*(Each moonshot in the full essay also carries benchmarks, milestone dates, guardrails, and spillover theory — not fully reproduced here; see source for per-mission detail.)*

## 4. Stakeholder playbook — “Build the Rails” (Chapter 9)

**Thesis:** Abundance arrives when we build rails (scorecards, data trusts, action networks, escrowed compute, outcome contracts, decision logs) and aim them — not by inspiration alone.

### Ten gears of the Abundance Engine
1. Build the Targeting System  
2. Pay for Results  
3. Pre-commit Compute (escrow unlocked by clears)  
4. Build Action Networks (shared robotic labs / micro-factories)  
5. Pool Data (Data Trusts)  
6. Demand Logs (DR-AIS / immutable why)  
7. Two-Source Rule (two independent AIs for critical decisions)  
8. Co-locate Power & Intelligence  
9. Fairness by Design (in the paid targets)  
10. Teach the Rules (civic literacy: read targets + check logs)

### Role playbooks (source labels) — highlights for founders / operators / BD

**Investor (maps to founders raising / BD selling rails):**  
- Shift Venture Capital → **Infrastructure Capital**; be the “Armorer.”  
- 90 days: launch a **Rails Fund** — only primitives (targeting platforms, audits, data trusts, action networks).  
- Year 1: stop funding “AI for X” apps; fund sector industrialization.  
- Alpha: **Own the Primitives** — apps copyable; toll roads (rails) durable.

**Industrial Leader / CTO (operators):**  
- 90 days: target **24-hour delivery** on key products → force local automated manufacturing.  
- 6 months: **two-source** rule for safety-critical stacks (two independent AI codebases).  
- Alpha: **Buy proofs, not hours** — pay for bug-free mathematical proofs, not consulting time.

**Health System Operator:**  
- Publish baselines; install secure action pathways for AI.  
- Procure results (e.g. 10% readmission cut); tie AI authority to live safety score.  
- Alpha: automate evaluation before action; revoke authority on slip.

**Infrastructure Operator:**  
- Define Reliability Target (outage minutes avoided).  
- Contracts treating data centers as **virtual batteries** / grid stabilizers (throttle for cheaper rates).

**Mayor / Governor (operators of physical reality):**  
- City Outcome Ledger (water, power, learning velocity, hospital waits).  
- Pilot 24h municipal micro-factory, district AI tutors.  
- Convert ~25% procurement to outcomes (“pothole-free days” not road-work hours).  
- City can be its own Targeting Authority without waiting for federal.

**National Leader:**  
- Aim national cognition; top Moonshots consensus in 90 days; National Capability Account; Compute-for-Outcomes Auctions; shift R&D from proposals → prizes.

**Citizen / “Governor of the system”:**  
- Join data trusts; host neighborhood outcome-ledger nights.  
- Ask every powerful system: *What benchmark governs this decision, and what happens automatically if it fails?*

### What to stop (innovation theater)
Stop: proposal demos with no follow-through; vanity scorecards; paying for PDFs/hours/pilots without outcome clauses; single-provider critical AI; secret model changes without decision logs; data systems without automatic safety slow-down.  
**Boards:** tie exec comp to verified outcome clears + safety uptime, not slideware.

### Before Monday noon (actionable checklist)
1. Pick **one** outcome metric you own; publish it (even privately).  
2. Draft one-page **Target Charter**.  
3. Identify one partner; propose a tiny outcome-based contract.  
4. Open a **Compute Escrow** budget line (even conceptual) with clear unlock conditions.  
5. Template your first **Decision Log** (record the “why”).

### Funding reallocation (not new money)
Nations: ~1% budgets → outcome procurement + rail building. Cities: ~10% capital budgets → proven results. Philanthropy: ~half programs → payment-for-clear prizes.

### Coaching angle for founders / BD / relationship roles
- Sell **rails and primitives**, not model apps.  
- Productize: Targeting Authorities, blinded eval harnesses, Data Trusts, Action Network access, Compute Escrow, Outcome Procurement contracts, DR-AIS logging, Two-Source verification.  
- BD narrative: “We write the scorecard / own the toll road everyone must run on.”  
- Relationship work: convene experts for 3–7 public metrics; escrow prizes; multi-party data trusts; city/hospital outcome ledgers.  
- Personal brand shift: from hero solver → **Harness Builder** / Conductor of Intelligence / Explorer of Purpose.
